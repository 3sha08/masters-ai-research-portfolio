# Master's AI Research Portfolio

## Overview

Welcome to my Master's AI Research Portfolio. This repository showcases a collection of research-driven projects completed as part of my graduate studies in Information Technology Management at Webster University. The projects focus on machine learning, Large Language Models (LLMs), Explainable AI (XAI), and AI-assisted decision support systems, with an emphasis on reproducible experimentation, model evaluation, and responsible AI.

This portfolio demonstrates my ability to design experiments, implement machine learning solutions, analyze results, and document research methodologies—skills that are directly applicable to AI research and AI alignment.

## Projects

### 1. AI-Assisted Decision Support Systems Using Large Language Models

**Objective**

Develop and evaluate an AI-powered decision support system that uses Large Language Models to analyze structured business data and generate actionable recommendations.

**Research Focus**

* Prompt engineering strategies
* Decision support using LLMs
* Response quality evaluation
* Hallucination analysis
* Responsible AI recommendations

### 2. Explainable AI for Machine Learning Models
